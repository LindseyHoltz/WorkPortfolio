Project Description

Where Glass Becomes Sea

A Creative Technologies capstone project by Lindsey Holtz under the guidance of Dr. Kristin Carlson.

Where Glass Becomes Sea is an interdisciplinary art project exploring ocean conservation through stained glass, projection mapping, animation, and interactive technology. Inspired by the beauty and fragility of marine ecosystems, the work combines traditional glassmaking techniques with digital media to create immersive installations that draw attention to endangered sea life and the impact humans have on the ocean.

The project focuses especially on sea turtles, inspired by personal experiences visiting the ocean and conservation centers in Pensacola and Navarre. These encounters sparked a desire to create artwork that celebrates marine life while encouraging viewers to reflect on the need for environmental protection.

The exhibition includes projection-mapped stained glass panels, animated stained-glass sequences, and interactive works that respond to touch. Through experimentation with light, texture, movement, and audience participation, the project explores how glass and technology can work together in new and meaningful ways. Themes of fragility, beauty, and human interaction are central to the work, with the materials themselves serving as metaphors for the vulnerability of ocean ecosystems.

By blending handcrafted processes with digital tools, Where Glass Becomes Sea aims to create memorable visual experiences that inspire curiosity, empathy, and a deeper appreciation for the natural world.
